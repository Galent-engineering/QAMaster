## Why
The 0.2.1 verification found five small bugs. Three of them (B1, B2, B4) have to be fixed before the 219 path-tag updates are applied to ADO. Otherwise every update rewrites descriptions, and passwords from `test_data` go along with them.

## Before you start
- `main` has 0.2.1 locally (not pushed). The branch is `feature/0.2.2-fixes`, cut from `main`.
- One commit per item, each test-first. Nothing is pushed.
