## Why
After 0.3.2 a demo ran from install to a verdict, then ended without a report. Setup still wanted flags, the DSM dropped some forms, and a crawl could die when the browser closed. The person asked for the fixes and the missing pieces together, to show the full picture.

## What shipped
Built on `feature/0.3.2-toolshop-fixes`, test-first. The crawler, setup and reports work ran in three parallel sessions, each on its own branch, and was replayed here without merge commits so history stays linear.
